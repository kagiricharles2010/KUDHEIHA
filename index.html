Scope and disclaimer: Unofficial demo based on the KUDHEIHA Return to Work Formula schedules signed on 30 October 2026. Union dues are fixed at 2%. Nothing you enter is stored or sent, and the form clears when you refresh or leave the page. Leave allowance (Schedule II) is unchanged by the agreement and paid once a year (36 working days of leave from 1 Jan 2027), so it isn't in arrears or monthly pay. The commuter phase-in timing isn't specified in the RTWF, so the option you choose is applied; arrears include the commuter increase.
